# Run a review game

The Review widget plays any quiz in your library as a live game. Quiz stays for real assessments; Review is for practice.

## Add Review to your board

Open the widget library and add **Review**. It shows the same quiz library as the Quiz widget, so there is nothing to copy.

## Start a game

1. Click **Start** on a quiz.
2. Pick a mode:
   - **Teacher-paced**: one question at a time on the board. Everyone answers, then you show the results before moving on. Turn on **Advance automatically** to move on once everyone has answered.
   - **Self-paced game**: students work through the questions on their own devices until time runs out. A missed question comes back later.
3. For a self-paced game, set the **Game length**.
4. Pick your classes and how many names the board shows: Top 5, Top 10 or Everyone.
5. Click **Start**.

Questions that need a teacher grade, such as written answers, are left out of the game. The dialog tells you how many.

## During a self-paced game

Students join with the code, as they do for a quiz. The board shows the time left, how many students have joined and are active, first-try accuracy, and the leaderboard.

- **Start game** starts the clock.
- **+1 min** adds a minute.
- **Pause** stops the clock and students' devices until you resume.
- **Names** shows or hides student names on the board.
- **End game** stops it for everyone.

Students see right or wrong after each answer and their final rank when the game ends.

## After the game

Finished games move to the Review archive with the final ranking, points and first-try accuracy for each question. Review games are practice: they have no grades to publish and don't go to the gradebook.
