# Edit logs are deleted each school year

The edit log behind a Writing record (every edit a student made, deleted text included) is deleted each 1 August, for every piece with no writing since 1 July. A piece still being written over the summer keeps its log until the next 1 August. The turned-in text, its feedback and the record's summary numbers (time spent, Writing sessions, pastes and untyped text with their content, time away) stay as long as quiz responses do. We chose this over keeping logs as long as the text, because the keystrokes are the most personal thing SpartBoard holds about a student and only matter while a grade can still be disputed, and over a clock counted from the grade, which could remove a log mid-year while a teacher and student are still talking about the work.

## Consequences

- After its log is deleted a Writing record shows its summary and says playback is no longer kept.
- A teacher who needs playback as evidence past the summer has to act before then; SpartBoard offers no way to keep one log.
- A piece's edit log is also capped at about 2 MB. Past that, recording stops and the teacher is told; the student keeps writing.
- Deleting a Writing assignment deletes its responses, frozen copies and logs at once.
