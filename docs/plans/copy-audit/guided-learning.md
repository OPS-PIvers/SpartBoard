# Copy audit: Guided Learning, Help Center, live tours (area "gl")

## 1. Summary

The Studio editor (flag `gl-studio`) is the tightest surface in the area. It is fully i18n'd, mostly one-line hints, and the tour runner copy is short. The permanent copy problem sits in three places. The first is the Studio properties panel, where every toggle and every choice group carries a grey sentence under it, often restating the option name ("Off" → "Markers stay still."). The second is the live-tour authoring panels (admin-only), which stack a hint under every heading. The third is the legacy teacher-facing surfaces (classic editor, AI generator, screen-capture modal, capture menu, pulse/transition chips), which are hard-coded English with em dashes, "Gemini will analyze..." narration and implementation detail. The worst single strings are the classic editor's "Always hide hotspot marker" `title` tooltip (a 70-word paragraph about render internals), the AI generator intro paragraph, the screen-capture modal's per-mode hint paragraph, and the recorder start dialog (three stacked paragraphs plus a "Tip:"). Student-facing chrome is mostly fine, but the class-picker screen has two helper lines that restate its heading, and the paused overlay needs three sentences to say one thing.

## 2. Top offenders

1. **Classic step editor, hide-marker row** (`components/widgets/GuidedLearning/components/GuidedLearningStepEditor.tsx:145-158`). The permanent sub-line reads "Marker never appears. Useful for "find the click zone" exercises in explore mode." The row also has a ~70-word `title` tooltip explaining render timing ("suppresses the brief flash before the interaction renders"). Implementation narrative leaking into the UI.
2. **AI generator intro** (`GuidedLearningAIGenerator.tsx:437-445`). A permanent paragraph at the top of the panel: "Add one or more images — upload, paste, or pull from Drive. Gemini will analyze them together and draft a guided learning experience with hotspots spanning the images you provide." It restates the upload button right below it, names the vendor, uses an em dash, and says "experience". The dropzone adds two more lines ("Click to upload, drop here, or paste (Ctrl+V)", "PNG, JPG, WebP — multi-select supported").
3. **Screen capture modal header** (`ScreenCaptureModal.tsx:52-58`, rendered at :314). Each mode shows a two-sentence paragraph under its title, e.g. "Open a video file, scrub to a moment, and extract it as an image slide — or add the whole clip as a video slide." The extractor missed these because they live in a const map. The empty stage adds "Pick the window or tab with your workflow" under the "Share your screen" button.
4. **Recorder start dialog** (`recorder/RecordingSession.tsx:248-264`, admin/live-tours). Two bullet paragraphs plus a "Tip:" line sit under one input: "You record on your own board. Student names from your classes, and student photos, video and work, are blurred in every frame as it is captured." / "Before anything uploads, you check every frame..." / "Tip: record on a board whose classes come from test classes, so few real names are on screen." The privacy promise is justified, but this is three paragraphs where one short line would do.
5. **Studio Activity panel** (`studio/StudioPropertiesPanel.tsx:287-352`). Four ChoiceGroups each show a description line for the selected option. The welcome toggle has a hint ("Students see your message before they start, and the Start button reads "Get started"."). A trailing "Select a step on the slide or in the timeline to edit it." is always shown when slides exist. So up to six grey sentences sit permanently in one panel.
6. **Studio live-tour step controls** (`studio/StudioTourControls.tsx:170, 285`, admin). The "Live tour" heading has the hint "Link this step to the real button so the activity can run live on a board." The checkbox has "Guided tours show the click, then wait for the teacher to do it." The Tour setup and Publish sections add two more hints (`StudioTourSetup.tsx:50`, `StudioTourPublish.tsx:112`). Four hint sentences stack in one group.
7. **Classic editor WelcomeChip popover** (`editorShared/WelcomeChip.tsx:108-113`). "Replaces the default mode/step subtitle on the student start screen with your custom message and changes the Start button to "Get started"." Mechanism narration with internal terms ("mode/step subtitle").
8. **Classic editor pulse/transition chips and capture menu** (`editorShared/setOptions.ts:28-60`, `editorShared/CaptureMenuButton.tsx:27-39`). Each dropdown option carries a sentence, several with em dashes: "No animation — hotspots stay still.", "New image slides in from the right; previous image exits left.", "Continuous breathing pulse so hotspots are always discoverable."
9. **Results "view-only share" state** (`Widget.tsx:1491-1499`). An em-dash title plus a two-sentence explanation that ends by pointing to another screen ("URL open counts appear in the Shared archive.").
10. **Help Center footer** (`components/help/HelpCenterModal.tsx:192`). "Press Ctrl/⌘ + / anytime to open Help" is shown permanently inside Help, and the Shortcuts tab above it already lists "Open Help".
